01 — About

“Our giri is for Gandhinagar. Our guri is a prosperous Karnataka.”

His pledge — giri, the hill; guri, the goal

Sapthagiri Gowda A R leads the Bharatiya Janata Party's Bengaluru Central District — the organisation covering the old heart of the city, from the Majestic interchange to the markets of Chickpet and Cottonpet.

He was named district president on 14 January 2024 by state president B Y Vijayendra, one of 39 district heads appointed ahead of that year's Lok Sabha elections. By his own account, he had earlier served as General Secretary of the same district and as president of the BJP Yuva Morcha for Bengaluru City.

His electoral home is Gandhi Nagar. He contested the seat for the BJP in 2018 and again in 2023, raising his vote from 37,284 to 54,013 and finishing 105 votes behind the sitting MLA — the narrowest result anywhere in the state that year.

He lists his education as a B.Com and an LL.B, followed by postgraduate diplomas in Environmental Law from the National Law School of India and in Law from Staffordshire University in the United Kingdom.

    Heritage

    Gorata Smarak Abhiyan & Gorata Chalo

    Gorata, a village in Bidar district, is remembered as the Jallianwala Bagh of the South: in 1948 the Nizam's forces killed villagers there for hoisting the Indian flag. The BJP Yuva Morcha raised funds across Karnataka for a memorial — its foundation stone was laid on 17 September 2014, Hyderabad-Karnataka Liberation Day, and the memorial was inaugurated in 2023.

    He lists mobilising the Gorata Smarak Abhiyan and Gorata Chalo among his work.

    Background: Deccan Herald, 2014 · Deccan Herald, 2023

    Youth

    “Be Good – Do Good”

    Vivek Wrist Band programmes, timed to Swami Vivekananda's birth anniversary: young people wear the band and take up a social cause. Yuva Morcha units ran the campaign down to polling-booth level. He lists organising these programmes.

    Background: Deccan Herald, 2017

    Economy

    GST seminars

    Seminars explaining the Goods and Services Tax — of direct concern to a constituency built around wholesale trade.

    Relief

    After the cylinder tragedy

    He reports giving personal financial assistance to people affected by a gas-cylinder tragedy in the constituency.
